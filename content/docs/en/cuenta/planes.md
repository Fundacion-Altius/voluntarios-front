## What it is for

Choose and manage the organisation plan (Basic, Standard, or Full), see subscription status, and move from the free trial to a paid plan.

## Who uses it

- Organisation admins.

## Free trial

When the organisation is created, Klaruk starts a **15-day Full plan trial**, with no card required. There is only one trial per organisation.

During the trial:

- You have full access to Full-plan modules.
- Admin shows a banner with days remaining.
- You get a reminder email a few days before the end.

When the trial ends without a subscription:

- The organisation enters **read-only** mode for 30 days (view and export only; no create or edit).
- You can subscribe anytime from Plans to restore full access.
- `/docs` requires an active subscription or an ongoing trial.

## Step by step

1. Open **Plans** from the admin menu.
2. Choose Basic, Standard, or Full (monthly or annual).
3. Complete payment in Stripe Checkout.
4. After payment, status becomes active and read-only mode is cleared.

## What the agent can do

Ask the Klaruk assistant for shortcuts when enabled.

## Common issues

- If you do not see the menu, check your role and the organisation subscription.
- If you are read-only after the trial, subscribe from Plans; a second free trial is not started.
